Design Philosophy

Architectural integrity meets sensory restraint.

Trained at Columbia GSAPP with apprenticeships across Milan and Copenhagen, Jenny Waltz views an interior as an extension of urban geography. Manhattan spaces often suffer from either cold, sterile modernity or suffocating period pastiche. Waltz carves an unmistakable middle path: spaces that look contemporary yet feel hundreds of years deep.

Every commission begins with spatial surgery—correcting flow, unmasking natural daylight pathways, and framing views of water, brick, or high-canopy skies. Only then do we bring in bespoke architectural finishes crafted by our network of upstate millworkers, European stone cutters, and French bronze artisans.

01

Honest Sourcing

Unlacquered brass, vein-cut travertine, hand-planed French oak, and chalk-based mineral plasters that age gracefully.

02

Acoustic Serenity

Concealed soundproofing and heavy-drape thermal mass to shield the inner dwelling from New York's ambient friction.

03

Curated Provenance

Rare 20th-century Nordic, Italian, and Brazilian lighting paired with commissioned gallery-grade contemporary art.

Tactile Registry

The Material Language

Interact with our primary palette of raw earth, aged alloy, and woven fiber. These form the tactile baseline of every Jenny Waltz residence.

Origin: Tivoli, Italy

Tivoli Roman Travertine

Unfilled, brushed Roman travertine provides monumental weight and soothing texture. We specify massive continuous slab cuts for floating kitchen cantilevers, primary baths, and low-profile hearth plinths.

Aging Quality Softens with touch; develops natural satin patina
Application Monolithic Islands, Floating Fireplaces
Finish Wire-brushed & honed, open pore
