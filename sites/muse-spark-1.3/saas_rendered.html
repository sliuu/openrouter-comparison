Frequently asked questions

Will AI copy actually sound like our brand? +
Yes. Connected trains a private Brand Voice model on your website, past campaigns, and style guide. Teams report 98% voice match, and you can lock tone, banned words, and compliance rules.
Do we still control approvals? +
Completely. Nothing publishes without human approval unless you enable autopilot for specific low-risk channels. Full version history, comments, and audit logs are built in.
Is our data used to train public models? +
Never. Your data is isolated, encrypted, and never used to train shared models. We are SOC 2 Type II certified and GDPR compliant with EU data residency options.
How long does onboarding take? +
Most teams launch their first AI-built campaign in under a day. Import your brand in 10 minutes with our crawler, invite your team, and brief your first agent.
