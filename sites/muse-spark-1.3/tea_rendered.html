01 — OUR PHILOSOPHY
Old Kyoto, softened for San Francisco.

We keep the bow, the whisk, the silence — but we added outlets, oat milk, and chairs you actually want to sit in for three hours. This is chadō for modern life.

🍃

Ichi-go ichi-e, daily.

One time, one meeting. Whether it's a 6-minute takeaway or a 90-minute ceremony, we treat your bowl as unrepeatable. Tea is sourced directly from three family farms in Uji, Shizuoka & Yame.

☁️

Designed for cafe-sitting.

Low hinoki tables, tatami nooks, plus a sunlit communal bar with fast wifi and slow tea refills. Linger. Read. Fog-watch. Our staff will never nudge your cup. Kanso — simplicity — with cushions.
