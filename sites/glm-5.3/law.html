What we fight

Negligence comes in many forms. So does our answer.

Michigan's laws are unlike any other state's — no-fault thresholds, damage caps, government notice deadlines. We have spent four decades learning exactly where the traps are set, and who set them.

Rear-endings on the Lodge, jackknifed semis on I-94, dooring crashes on Woodward. Michigan's no-fault system is unlike any other state's — and insurers know exactly how to use it against you. We know how to use it for you.

How it works

After you call.

No mystery, no runaround. Here is exactly what happens — and what never does.

Step 01

A lawyer calls you back. Today.

Not a call center, not a "client services coordinator." An attorney reviews your situation and tells you honestly whether you have a case — including, sometimes, the answer you'd rather not hear: no.

Step 02

We take the case — and the costs.

Sign nothing you don't understand; we explain everything first. From that hour, every expense is ours: records, reconstruction, experts, depositions. You never write us a check.

Step 03

We prepare for trial.

We build the file as if twelve strangers will decide it — because they might. Filing suit isn't a tactic here; it's leverage, and adjusters price our files accordingly.

Step 04

You recover. Literally.

Verdict or settlement, the fee is the same: a percentage, only if we win. You focus on healing. We handle everything else — and we mean everything.
