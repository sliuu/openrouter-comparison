01 — Philosophy

A home is not decorated.
It is composed — over time, with intent, around the life that happens inside it.

For over fifteen years, Jenny has practiced a discipline of subtraction: fewer objects, better chosen; fewer gestures, more deeply felt. Her rooms favor natural materials, honest patina, and light treated as the most valuable material of all.

03 — The Process

I.

Conversation

Every project begins around a table — understanding how you live, gather, rest, and retreat before a single drawing is made.

II.

Composition

Concepts, materials, and custom millwork developed into a coherent architectural language for your home.

III.

Craft

Collaboration with master artisans and fabricators across the city, with Jenny on site through every milestone.

IV.

Reveal

A considered installation, styled to the last linen — then left alone, to be lived in beautifully.
