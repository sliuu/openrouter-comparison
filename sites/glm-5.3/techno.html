The Signal

Why a forest

Three days of low end threaded through one thousand hectares of pine, birch and open sky — twenty-two minutes from Amsterdam Zuid.

Entanglement started in 2019 as a single soundsystem strapped between two oaks and forty people who didn't want to go home. Six years later the roots have spread: three stages cut into the treeline of the Amsterdamse Bos, tuned to sit under the canopy instead of above it.

The forest does something a warehouse can't. It swallows the city at the gate and hands back bass that arrives like weather. We build the floor around the trees that are already there — nothing cleared that didn't ask for it, nothing left behind that doesn't compost.

    Terrain

    Practicalities

    A clearing cut into the treeline

    Three systems, one flow. The Root System carries the hard groove. Canopy is the dense, drone-leaning middle. The Clearing opens at sunset for the long, slow ones. Follow the rope lights, not the map.

    House rules // read twice
    • 01No photos on the dancefloor. Live inside it — your memory is the archive.
    • 02No glitter, no confetti. The moss remembers everything.
    • 03Leave no trace. Pack out what you pack in, down to the filter tips.
    • 04Respect the canopy — no climbing, no fires, no shortcuts through the undergrowth.
    • 05If someone looks lost, you're their crew now. That's the whole policy.
    Gate 03
    Amstelveenseweg side. Opens 14:00 daily, last entry 04:00. The rope lights start at the bike racks.
    Transit
    Shuttle every 20 minutes from Amsterdam Zuid (22 min ride, night service until 07:00). 3,000 bike racks. No car parking — seriously.
    Bivouac
    A marked pitch zone under the pines. Tents only, low-fi, quiet hours 08:00–12:00. Rented two-person tents while stock lasts.
    Sound
    14:00 until curfew at 06:00 sharp. After that the birds take over, and honestly, they're not bad.
    Conditions
    Forest floor: boots over sneakers. It rained in 2023. It will rain again.
    Age
    18+. ID at the gate. No exceptions, no hard-luck stories.
