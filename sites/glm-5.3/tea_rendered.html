考え方

Tradition, measured by the minute, not the ceremony.

Founded by third-generation tea merchants from Kyoto, Koyo — “autumn leaves” — is a bridge between two speeds of life: the unhurried precision of the tea room, and the honest need of San Franciscans for a place to sit, work, and simply be.

Our matcha is stone-milled in Uji each week and whisked to order. Our sweets are made in-house each morning. Wifi is free, laptops are welcome — but the tatami room asks, gently, for stillness.
