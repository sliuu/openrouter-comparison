Manifesto

Why we go into the trees

Techno was never meant for the club alone. It was built for rooms that don't exist yet — and a forest at 4 a.m., lit only by a single red lamp and two thousand people moving as one, is the closest thing we've found. Entanglement is a festival built around what happens when the sound, the ground and the crowd stop being separate things.

  1. I

    Sound first. Each stage is tuned on-site by the system designers over three nights before doors. No headliner-first booking. The sonic arc of the weekend is written before a single name is confirmed.

  2. II

    Darkness is a feature. Minimal light, no LED walls, no lasers. You will see the trees, the fog and the people next to you. That's it.

  3. III

    Phones stay in your pocket. Cameras are covered at the gate with a sticker. Anyone filming on the floor is asked to leave. What happens here is for the people here.

  4. IV

    Leave the forest as we found it. Zero single-use plastic, compost toilets, a full ecological survey before and after. The site is chosen with the landowner and Staatsbosbeheer, and it changes every year.

  5. V

    Everyone is a guest. Zero tolerance for harassment. Awareness team in violet, always reachable. If you don't feel safe, we haven't done our job.

Q.01Where exactly is the forest?
Somewhere in the woodlands north of Amsterdam, about 35 minutes by road from Centraal. The exact coordinates are sent to ticket holders 48 hours before doors, along with your shuttle slot. We do this to protect the site and to keep the weekend ours. You will not find it on Instagram.
Q.02How do I get there?
Shuttles run from Amsterdam Sloterdijk every 20 minutes from Thu 17:00 through Mon 10:00 and are included in every ticket. There is no car park at the site and the access road is closed to private traffic. Bikes are welcome — a marked route is released with the coordinates, and there's a guarded bike field at the entrance.
Q.03What's the deal with phones?
Your camera lenses are stickered at the gate. Phones are allowed on site for messaging and emergencies, but not on the dancefloors. Anyone filming or photographing on a floor will be asked to leave and their ticket cancelled without refund. We've had zero leaked images in four editions. Help us keep it that way.
Q.04Is there food, water, warmth?
Free drinking water at eight points across the site. Six food stalls — all vegetarian, most vegan, open around the clock. Two fire pits near Canopy and a heated tent at the camp. It's a Dutch forest in August: expect 12°C at 5 a.m. and rain at least once. Dress like you mean it.
Q.05Safety & awareness
Our awareness team wears violet and is present at every stage and the camp, 24 hours a day. A medical post with paramedics sits between Root and Canopy. Drug checking is available on site via a partner service, anonymous and free. If something feels wrong, find violet — or text the number on your wristband.
Q.06Accessibility
Root and Canopy are reachable via hardened paths and have raised viewing platforms. Undergrowth, being a wartime bunker, has steps and a low doorway and is not step-free. Accessible camping, toilets and a dedicated shuttle are available — write to access@entanglement.fest and we'll sort it with you directly.
