Michigan Law, Explained

Michigan's no-fault system is different. Your lawyer should be, too.

Michigan is one of the few states with a no-fault auto insurance system — and the 2019 reforms made it more complex, not less. Choosing the wrong PIP coverage level or missing a filing deadline can permanently cost you benefits.

We've handled thousands of Michigan no-fault claims. We know the deadlines, the exceptions, and the tactics adjusters use to deny "reasonably necessary" care.

1 yr
Deadline to file for no-fault PIP benefitsYou must submit written notice of your claim to your insurer within one year of the accident to preserve benefits.
3 yrs
Statute of limitations for injury lawsuitsMost Michigan personal injury and wrongful death claims must be filed within three years — but exceptions can shorten this.
51%
Modified comparative fault thresholdIf you're found more than 50% at fault, you cannot recover non-economic damages. Evidence preservation is critical.
$5k
Mini-tort limit for vehicle damageSince 2020, you can recover up to $5,000 in vehicle damage from an at-fault driver — separate from your injury claim.
