The healthy-ish promise

We didn't reinvent candy. We just cleaned up its act.

Sugar-free candy tastes like a compromise. Ours tastes like the real thing — because it is — with fewer of the ingredients you'd rather not squint at.

Real cane sugar

No sugar alcohols, no aftertaste, no tummy trouble. Just about 30% less sugar than the big-brand stuff.

Actual fruit

Watermelon, peach, mango, cherry — as purée and juice, not a lab's best guess at what fruit smells like.

Colors from plants

Beet, turmeric, spirulina, and butterfly pea. Bright enough for kids, boring enough for pediatricians.

Small batches, fresh

Made weekly in Santa Barbara and shipped within days. Candy has a season too — it's now.

Read the label. We dare you.

Everything in the jar, and everything we keep out of it.

Our ingredient lists are short enough to read aloud to a toddler. We've tried. They lose interest by "cane sugar," which is fair.

✓ Always in

  • 🌾
    Organic cane sugarThe real deal, used sparingly. Sweetness is a feature, not a bug.
  • 🍑
    Fruit purée & juice concentrateMost flavors are 20–35% real fruit by weight.
  • 🍋
    Citric & malic acidFrom citrus and apples. That's the puckery part.
  • 🌱
    Pectin & tapiocaPlant-based chew. Gelatin-free across the whole line.
  • 🎨
    Beet, turmeric, spirulinaNature's crayon box. Sometimes slightly less neon. We're fine with it.

✕ Never in

  • 🚫
    High-fructose corn syrupNot once, not ever, not even in the sour belts.
  • 🚫
    Artificial dyes (Red 40, Yellow 5, Blue 1…)Our reds come from beets and our blues from butterfly pea flowers.
  • 🚫
    Sugar alcohols & artificial sweetenersNo erythritol, sucralose, or aspartame. Your gut will thank us.
  • 🚫
    Hydrogenated oils & palm oilA little coconut oil keeps chews from sticking. That's it.
  • 🚫
    The top 9 allergensMade in a dedicated nut-, dairy-, and gluten-free kitchen.

Healthy-ish is a vibe, not a medical claim. Candy is still candy. Please enjoy responsibly, and share with someone you like.
