The platform

One workspace where humans and agents share the board.

See every campaign, every asset, and every agent decision in one place. Approve with a click, edit inline, and never lose the thread.

  • Campaign board

    Track assets from plan to live. Agents move cards as they work; you see exactly what needs a human eye.

  • Brand memory

    Voice, visual identity, claims library, and past winners — learned once, applied everywhere, versioned like code.

  • Approval & governance

    Role-based approvals, legal review queues, and a full audit trail of every agent action. Nothing ships without a sign-off.

  • Closed-loop learning

    Results flow back from your channels so the Strategist gets smarter with every launch — not just every model update.
