Our approach

A room should feel like a long exhale — never decorated, only resolved.

Every project begins with a series of long conversations before a single swatch is chosen. We want to know how you start your mornings, where you read, which wall you watch the light move across. The drawings follow the life — not the other way around.

From there, we compose in layers: proportion, then material, then the quiet imperfections that make a space feel collected rather than bought. The result is a home that photographs beautifully and, more importantly, gets better with use.

The studio

Fifteen years of listening first.

Jenny Waltz founded the studio in 2009 after a decade in the architecture offices of two of New York's most respected firms. What began as a single gut renovation on Jane Street has grown into a practice of six designers working on residences throughout the city and the Hudson Valley.

The studio is deliberately small. Jenny leads every project personally, from the first walkthrough to the day the last cushion is placed, and we take on no more than eight commissions a year so that each one gets the attention a home deserves.
