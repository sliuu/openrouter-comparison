// 01 — The idea

A temporary
autonomous
zone in the trees.

Entanglement is not a festival in the way you're used to. There is no main sponsor, no branded stage, no photo wall. There is a clearing, a sound system, and roughly four thousand people who came for the same reason.

For 72 hours the forest becomes the venue. Four stages sit between the birches and the water — one of them, The Chapel, is a converted pump house with a single strobe and no ceiling. You will walk. You will get lost. That's part of it.

We book artists we actually listen to, we keep the sound clean, and we leave the woods exactly as we found them. Nothing more, nothing less.

  • No phones on the dancefloor
  • No single-use plastic on site
  • No headliners — only residents and guests
  • No leaving without taking your tent

// 03 — Practical

Everything you
need to know.

The exact coordinates are released to ticket holders 48 hours before gates. Getting there is easy. Getting back is your own problem.

// Location

Amsterdamse Bos

A 1,000-hectare forest 20 minutes from Amsterdam Centraal. Free shuttle buses run from Amsterdam Zuid station every 30 minutes, from 14:00 until 08:00.

// Capacity

4,000 people

Deliberately small. We cap the site at four thousand so the dancefloors never collapse into a queue and you never lose your friends for good.

// Hours

72 hours

Music runs non-stop from Friday 16:00 until Sunday 23:00. Camping stays open until Monday 12:00 for those who need a soft landing.

// Sound

Four stages

Moss Stage, Root Stage, The Pit and The Chapel. All rigs are tuned on-site, sound-capped at 102 dB, and rebuilt every morning before doors.

// Camping

Sleep in the trees

Forest camping is included with every weekend ticket. Showers, water points and a 24-hour quiet zone are on site. Bring a real tent — July in NL rains.

// Care

Harm reduction

A trained welfare and first-aid crew is on site around the clock, no questions asked. Free water, earplugs and sunscreen at every stage.

// 05 — FAQ

Questions,
answered.

Where exactly is Entanglement?

The grounds are inside the Amsterdamse Bos, just south of Amsterdam. Because the site is protected woodland, we only release the exact gate coordinates to ticket holders by email 48 hours before gates open. Shuttle buses from Amsterdam Zuid station run every 30 minutes and take roughly 15 minutes — no car parking is available on site except for accessible parking, which you can request when you buy.

Is camping really included?

Yes — every Weekend and Forest Ritual ticket includes forest camping from Friday 16:00 until Monday 12:00. Campsites are quiet after 07:00, with water points, showers and charging lockers. There is a separate 24-hour quiet zone for light sleepers. Day pass holders cannot camp and must leave before 08:00.

Why can't I use my phone on the dancefloor?

Because the point is the music. At the gate we place a small removable sticker over your phone camera, and phone use is limited to the camping and chill areas. Security will politely ask you to put it away if you film a set. We photograph the festival ourselves and publish a free gallery afterwards.

Is the site accessible?

Most of it, yes. The main paths are compacted and wheelchair-accessible, and the Moss and Root stages have level viewing platforms. The Chapel and The Pit sit on natural forest floor and are harder to reach — we run a free on-site shuttle between stages for anyone who needs it. Email access@entanglement.nl and we'll arrange everything before you arrive.

What's the policy on drugs and alcohol?

Dutch law applies on site. There is zero tolerance for hard drugs and anyone caught dealing is removed immediately. Our harm-reduction team is on site 24 hours a day and will help anyone in trouble without judgement. Free water is available at every stage — please actually drink it.

Can I leave and come back?

Weekend and Forest Ritual tickets include unlimited re-entry between 07:00 and 23:00 each day. Day pass holders get one re-entry. Bring your ticket and ID every time — wristbands alone are not enough.

What should I pack?

Waterproof boots, a real tent with a groundsheet, a warm layer for the 04:00 hours, a torch with a red light, refillable bottle, and earplugs (free at the gate, but bring your own if you're fussy). Leave glass, fireworks and drones at home — they will be confiscated.
