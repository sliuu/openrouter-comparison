Why real sugar

Honest candy, made the slow way

We started Sweet Summer Days because we got tired of reading candy labels that sounded like a science project. So we went back to the basics — and it turns out basics taste better.

Real cane sugar

Not corn syrup. We use cane sugar so every piece tastes clean and finishes sweet — never cloying.

Real fruit purée

Strawberries, lemons, raspberries and apple. If a flavour exists in nature, we use the nature version.

No artificial dyes

Colour comes from fruit, turmeric and beetroot. Yes, that's really how we do it.

Small batches

Cooked in 40-pound copper kettles in our Sonoma kitchen, tested by an unusually opinionated panel of kids.
