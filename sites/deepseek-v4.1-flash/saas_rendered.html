FAQ

Questions, answered.

Still curious? Our team replies in under four hours on weekdays.

Ask us anything
Will the agents actually sound like us?

Yes — that's the whole point. During onboarding you connect your style guide, top-performing content and a set of approved campaigns. Connected builds a brand voice profile that every agent writes against, and a reviewer agent scores each draft for tone, terminology and claims before it reaches your queue.

How do you handle data privacy and model training?

Your content is never used to train foundation models. Connected is SOC 2 Type II certified, GDPR compliant and supports EU data residency. Enterprise plans can route all inference through private model endpoints inside your own cloud tenancy.

Which tools does Connected integrate with?

Out of the box: HubSpot, Marketo, Salesforce, Pardot, Webflow, WordPress, Contentful, LinkedIn, Meta Ads, Google Ads, Slack and Notion. Anything else can be wired up through our REST API and webhooks, usually in an afternoon.

What does implementation look like?

Most teams are live in under a week. Day one is brand voice setup, day two connects your stack, and days three to five are a pilot campaign run alongside your existing process. Growth and Enterprise plans include a dedicated solutions architect throughout.

Do we still need copywriters?

Absolutely — they just stop doing first drafts. Teams typically redirect writing time toward strategy, positioning and the 20% of work that needs a human's judgement. Connected handles the volume; your team handles the taste.
