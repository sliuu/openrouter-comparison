A cup for every kind of day

Find your kind of tea.

New to Japanese tea? Start with how you’d like to feel. There’s no wrong place to begin.

Bright & a little bold → Matcha

Shade-grown leaves, stone-ground into a vibrant powder. Full-bodied, grassy, and gently sweet. Try it straight for the full experience, or as a latte for a softer start.

Cozy & mellow → Hojicha

Roasted green tea with notes of toasted nuts and caramel. Naturally lower in caffeine, it’s our favorite for a slower afternoon.

Fresh & quietly complex → Sencha

A classic Japanese green tea. Delicate, refreshing, and a little savory — beautiful on its own, one small sip at a time.

Still curious? Ask us at the counter. We love talking tea.
