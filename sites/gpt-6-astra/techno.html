04 / Before you disappear

Good to
know.

A few things before you follow the sound.
Still curious? Say hello ↗

Where are we heading?

Into the Amsterdamse Bos, just outside Amsterdam. The exact entrance and site map will be shared with ticket holders before the festival. Think tall trees, open clearings, and room to breathe.

How do I get there?

Come by bike or public transport where possible. Festival shuttles will run from Amsterdam Zuid; the timetable and shuttle booking details will be announced closer to the weekend. Please don’t drive into the forest.

What's the camping situation?

Bring your own tent and settle in. The Weekend + Camping passage includes three nights, from Friday to Monday, with drinking water, toilets, and hot showers. Camping opens Friday at 10:00 and closes Monday at 12:00. Day and regular weekend passages do not include camping.

Everyone belongs. What does that mean?

All identities, all backgrounds, one dance floor. No discrimination, harassment, or unwanted attention. Consent is non-negotiable. Our awareness crew will be on site throughout the festival. Look after yourself, each other, and the forest.

Can I put my phone away?

Yes, please. We encourage a no-camera dance floor so everyone can be present and feel free. Keep your phone for meeting friends and getting home, not recording someone else’s moment.

What should I bring?

A valid photo ID (18+), your ticket, comfortable shoes, layers for the night, ear protection, and a reusable water bottle. If you’re camping, bring your tent and sleeping gear. Leave glass, disposable glitter, and bad attitudes at home.
