How it works

From a one-line brief to a live campaign

You stay the marketer. Connected removes the 40 hours of assembly in between.

Connect your context

Sync your site, docs, CRM, ad accounts and past campaigns. Connected builds a living brand memory — voice, ICP, offers, claims and what already worked.

Brief your agents

Describe the goal in plain language or drop in a brief. Agents plan the campaign, propose segments and channels, and surface assumptions for you to approve.

Review, launch, learn

Approve in one click. Connected pushes assets to your channels, tracks performance, and rewrites the underperformers automatically next cycle.
