01 / CONCEPT

Entanglement began in 2019 as a 200-person generator party under a highway bridge in Amsterdam-Noord. Seven years later we are still allergic to VIP decks, LED walls and 45-second drops.

Instead: a working forestry estate east of the city, borrowed for 72 hours. Five sound systems hidden between the pines, all of them pointed inward. One long set instead of six short ones. A programme built on resident-led marathon slots, live hardware and the kind of records you only hear at 05:40.

Phones stay in your pocket on the dancefloor — camera lenses are covered at the gate. What happens in the trees is remembered, not recorded.

03 / STAGES
S/01

The Hollow

A natural bowl in the forest floor, ringed by 40-year-old pines. Funktion-One stacks buried in the earthworks, no stage riser — the booth sits at ground level in the middle of the crowd.

Main floor · 2,200 cap · 4-point sound
S/02

Ferrous

A decommissioned grain silo on the estate edge. Concrete, rebar, seven seconds of natural reverb. Hard, fast, industrial. Strobe-only lighting; ear protection handed out at the door.

Industrial · 700 cap · 140–155 BPM
S/03

Mycelium

Deep in the undergrowth, 34 small speakers strung through the canopy. Ambient, dub techno and lying down. Open continuously for all 72 hours. Whispering only.

Ambient · 300 cap · Distributed array
S/04

Observatory

On the only clearing high enough to see the sky. Faces east, opens at 04:00 each night and closes when the sun clears the treeline. Sunrise sets, electro and long unmixed passages.

Sunrise · 900 cap · 04:00 — 09:00
S/05

Bunker 7

A WWII bunker, 60 m². Rave capacity: 120 wet bodies. Curated nightly by a different Amsterdam collective. No line-up published in advance. You either find it or you don't.

Secret · 120 cap · Unannounced

04 / INFO

Getting there

Train + shuttle: Amsterdam Centraal → Vosbergen-Zuid (34 min, every 20 min). Free electric shuttles run from the station to the gate every 15 minutes, Fri 15:00 – Mon 12:00.

Bike: guarded bike park for 1,200 bikes. 38 km of mostly flat riding along the Vecht. Bring a light.

Car: carpool-only parking (3+ people per vehicle). €25 per car, pre-booked.

Sleeping

The Pines — bring-your-own tent camping between the trees, no generators, quiet from 11:00 to 16:00.

The Clearing — pre-pitched 2p canvas tents with real mattresses, 400 available.

Showers are cold, plentiful and solar-heated by noon. Free drinking water everywhere. Campfires only in the four designated pits.

Care & conduct

Zero tolerance for racism, homophobia, transphobia and unwanted touching. Awareness team in acid-green vests roam all floors, all night — talk to them about anything.

Free drug-checking on site in partnership with a licensed harm-reduction service. Sober tent, quiet room and a medical post 90 seconds from every stage.

The forest

Vosbergen is a living production forest and a nesting site for tawny owls. Stay on marked paths. Sound curfew on outdoor stages 02:00 – 04:00 to protect nesting zones (indoor floors continue).

Everything you bring out, you carry back. €10 cup deposit, €20 tent deposit refunded on exit with an empty pitch.

06 / FAQ

On the dancefloors, yes. At the gate we place a small removable sticker over your camera lenses. Keep your phone for messages, maps and payments — just don't film. There are three designated photo spots near the food court if you need proof you were here.

Shuttles to Vosbergen-Zuid station run continuously from Friday 15:00 until Monday 12:00, including all night. Trains to Amsterdam Centraal run hourly through the night on Friday and Saturday. The full transport schedule is printed on the back of your wristband card.

Into the camping: your own food and sealed drinks (no glass, no gas canisters larger than 250 ml). Into the festival terrain: an empty water bottle, ear protection, warm layers. Nights in the forest drop to 11 °C even in August — people always underestimate this.

Three of five stages (The Hollow, Ferrous, Observatory) are reachable on firm compacted paths with a wheelchair or walker. Mycelium and Bunker 7 have soft or stepped access. Accessible camping, toilets and a raised viewing platform at The Hollow are available — email access@entanglement.fest and a human will answer within two days. Personal assistants enter free.

A small number of Saturday and Sunday day tickets (no camping, 12:00–02:00 access) are released on 20 July with the set times. The festival is designed as a continuous 72-hour arc, so we keep day tickets under 15% of capacity.

Then it rains for three days and Ferrous gets very busy. Two stages are fully covered, and all floors are laid with woodchip and drainage matting. Entanglement has never been cancelled or shortened due to weather. Bring boots.
