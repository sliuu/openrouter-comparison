Good questions

The fine print, in plain words

So... is this actually healthy?

It's candy. It's healthy-ish. We use real organic cane sugar (about 40% less than a conventional gummy), real fruit and vegetable juice for color, and nothing artificial. It's a treat made thoughtfully — not a supplement, not a snack bar, and definitely not a salad.

Why real sugar instead of a sugar substitute?

Because sugar alcohols and high-intensity sweeteners leave a cooling, chemical aftertaste and can upset sensitive stomachs — especially kids'. We'd rather use a little less of the real thing and let the fruit do more of the work.

Are there vegan and allergy-friendly options?

Yes. Every gummy flavor comes in a fruit-pectin version marked VG on the pouch. Everything we make is nut-free, gluten-free and dairy-free, produced in a facility that never handles peanuts or tree nuts.

How fast do you ship, and does it melt?

Orders leave our Santa Cruz kitchen within 1–2 business days, carbon-neutral. From June through September we tuck a cold pack into every box headed somewhere warm, free of charge. Free shipping on orders over $35.

What if my kid (or I) don't like it?

Tell us within 30 days and we'll refund you or swap the flavor — pouch open or not. The "Sweet Enough Guarantee," no receipt archaeology required.
