        <p class="lede">Koyo began as a question: what survives when a 400-year-old ritual moves into a storefront on Linden Street, between a bike shop and a laundromat?</p>
        <p>Not the formality. We let that go. What we kept is the attention — water measured by temperature rather than habit, leaf weighed rather than scooped, a second steep poured only when you're ready for it. Our tea comes from eleven family gardens in Uji, Wazuka, Kirishima and Yame, most of them small enough that we know the name of the person who picked it.</p>
        <p>The rest we built for here. Ceramic cups you don't have to be careful with. A long table with outlets. Oat milk. A room warm enough for fog. We think the tea house was always a public room first — a place to put down your day — and that translates just fine.</p>
      </div>

<section class="sec-pad ritual" id="ritual">
  <div class="wrap">
    <div class="two">
      <div class="reveal">
        <p class="kicker">Ritual · 作法</p>
        <h2 class="h1" style="margin-top:.7em;color:#F2EADB">Three steeps,<br>three teas.</h2>
      </div>
      <div class="reveal" style="--d:.1s">
        <p class="lede">The same leaf will give you a different cup each time you pour water over it. Here's what we tell people at the counter — take it home with you.</p>
      </div>
    </div>
    <div class="steps">
      <div class="step reveal">
        <span class="big">一</span>
        <h3>Cool the water</h3>
        <p>Boiling water scalds green leaf and pulls bitterness forward. Pour from the kettle into your empty cups first — every transfer drops the temperature about 10°C — then into the pot.</p>
        <div class="spec">Sencha 70°C · Gyokuro 55°C · Hojicha 95°C</div>
      </div>
      <div class="step reveal" style="--d:.08s">
        <span class="big">二</span>
        <h3>Weigh, don't scoop</h3>
        <p>Five grams of leaf to 150ml of water is our house ratio — roughly twice what a Western recipe suggests, steeped for half as long. Density varies wildly between teas; a scale removes the guesswork.</p>
        <div class="spec">5g : 150ml · 45 seconds</div>
      </div>
      <div class="step reveal" style="--d:.16s">
        <span class="big">三</span>
        <h3>Pour it all out</h3>
        <p>Empty the pot completely each time, down to the last drop — the leaf keeps brewing in whatever you leave behind. Second steep: hotter, faster, sweeter. Third: add thirty seconds and let it go soft.</p>
        <div class="spec">Steep 2 · +5°C, 20s &nbsp;— &nbsp;Steep 3 · +75s</div>
      </div>
    </div>
  </div>
</section>

      var r = cv.getBoundingClientRect();

        phase: Math.random()*Math.PI*2,
        rot: Math.random()*Math.PI*2,
        vr: (Math.random()-0.5)*0.012,
        c: COLORS[(Math.random()*COLORS.length)|0]
      };
    }
    function init(){
      size();
      var n = W < 700 ? 9 : 18;
      leaves = []; for(var i=0;i<n;i++) leaves.push(make(true));

    var t = 0;
    function frame(){
      t += 0.01;
      ctx.clearRect(0,0,W,H);
      for(var i=0;i<leaves.length;i++){
        var l = leaves[i];
        l.y += l.vy; l.rot += l.vr;
        var x = l.x + Math.sin(t*0.8 + l.phase) * l.sway;
        ctx.save();
        ctx.translate(x, l.y);
        ctx.rotate(l.rot);
        ctx.scale(l.s, l.s);
        ctx.translate(-50,-50);
        ctx.fillStyle = l.c;
        ctx.fill(LEAF);
        ctx.restore();
        if(l.y > H + 80) leaves[i] = make(false);
      }
      requestAnimationFrame(frame);
