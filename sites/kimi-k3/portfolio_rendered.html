The Studio

A waltz is counted in threes — proportion, light, texture. Jenny Waltz composes interiors the same way: measured, unhurried, and quietly unforgettable.

A studio built on rhythm.

Founded in 2009, the atelier accepts a small number of residences each year across Manhattan and Brooklyn. Every commission is led personally by Jenny — from the first measured walk-through to the final stem placed on the mantel.

The work is quiet on purpose. Rooms are composed the way music is written: structure first, ornament second, silence where it matters most.

The Method

Four movements.

01

Listen

Every commission begins with conversation — how you wake, gather, retreat. The brief is written in your routines.

02

Compose

Mood, material and proportion are arranged into a score: floor plans, palettes and a narrative for each room.

03

Refine

Drawings become samples, samples become rooms. Millwork is tuned, stone is selected slab by slab.

04

Reveal

A final styling pass, art hung to the millimetre, the keys returned — and the house begins its life.
