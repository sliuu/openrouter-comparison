05 / Protocol

Field Manual

The forest sits thirty minutes outside Amsterdam. Shuttle buses run every twenty minutes from Amstelstation from 18:00 on Friday until the last dancer leaves on Monday. A guided bike convoy departs Centraal Station at 19:00 sharp — lights provided, pace is militant. No private cars. No exceptions.

  • SHUTTLE — Amstelstation, every 20 min
  • BIKE CONVOY — Centraal, Fri 19:00
  • GPS — 52.2631°N, 5.1667°E

Sanctuary ticket holders sleep among the pines: pre-pitched bell tents with real mattresses, or bring your own and claim a pitch. Hot showers, a wood-fired sauna and a cold plunge pond sit at the camp's edge. Quiet hours from 08:00 — the forest sleeps, and so will you.

  • CHECK-IN — Fri from 14:00
  • CHECK-OUT — Mon 14:00
  • SAUNA — Book slots on site

Strictly 18+. No phones or cameras on any dancefloor — lenses are stickered at the gate, and the sticker stays on. No re-entry after 04:00. Respect the moss, the wildlife and each other. Consent is the headliner. Leave no trace — the forest was here first.

  • AGE — 18+ with ID
  • PHONES — Stickered at gate
  • RE-ENTRY — Until 04:00

The Mycelium Network — our roaming awareness team — is reachable at every stage, day and night. Free earplugs at all bars, free water everywhere, and an on-site drug-checking service run with health professionals. Look out for your entangled partners.

  • AWARENESS — 24/7 roaming team
  • DRUG CHECKING — On site, anonymous
  • EARPLUGS — Free at every bar

The entire site runs on solar and battery — no diesel generators. Cups carry a deposit, water points are unlimited, and every food vendor is plant-based. A percentage of each ticket funds rewilding of the very forest you are dancing in.

  • POWER — 100% solar + battery
  • FOOD — Fully plant-based
  • FOREST — Rewilding fund
