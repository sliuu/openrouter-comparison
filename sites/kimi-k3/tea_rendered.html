01 — Our Story 物語

Named for the turning of the leaves — the brief, blazing pause before the fall.

Koyo began with a simple question: what would a four-hundred-year-old tea house do about laptops? Our answer — nothing, and everything. We whisk matcha the slow way, brew sencha in small pots, and keep the wifi fast and the lighting kind.

The counter is hinoki cypress. The kettle is always on. Come for a meeting, a novel, a long catch-up, or nothing at all. In a city that rarely stops, we keep a slower hour — and a seat that is yours for as long as the light is good.

04 — The Slow Hour 点前

Twenty minutes. One bowl. No phones.

Every Friday through Sunday we clear the center table for a guided bowl of thick koicha matcha and a seasonal sweet. It isn't a performance — it's a pause, shared with seven strangers who won't stay strangers.

01来

Arrive

Leave the fog at the door. Shoes are optional; hurry is not. Your cushion is set before you sit.

02点

Whisk

Watch the bowl turn jade under eighty strokes of the chasen — then turn it twice, and drink.

03留

Stay

The seat is yours until the light changes. Most people close their laptops. Some just close their eyes.
