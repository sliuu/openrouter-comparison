The sweet fine print

What "healthy-ish" means here

01

Real sugar, honest amounts

We sweeten with real cane sugar — never corn syrup, never weird substitutes — and keep every serving at 12 grams or less. Sweet, not sneaky.

02

Fruit-first flavor

Our gummies start as actual juice. Our chews begin as real purée. If it tastes like a strawberry, a strawberry was involved. Revolutionary, we know.

03

Nothing artificial

Colors from beets, turmeric, and spirulina. Flavors from fruit. No dyes with numbers, no "natural flavor" mysteries, no lab-coat aftertaste.

04

Kind packaging

Compostable pouches, recyclable boxes, and shipping materials that won't outlive your great-grandchildren. The planet has a sweet tooth too.

05

Small batch, always

Every batch is cooked, poured, and packed by actual humans in our sunny little kitchen. Made weekly, shipped within 48 hours, gone fast.
